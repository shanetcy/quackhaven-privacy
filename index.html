Quackhaven: privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-19 · applies to the Android app "Quackhaven" (package com.shane.quackhaven) · 简体中文

In short

Quackhaven does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and the app does not ask for permission to use the internet.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your island: coins, buildings, residents, visitors, decorations, goals and story progress, in a save file.
  • Your settings: music and sound volume, language, and whether reminders are on.

Both live in the app's private storage on your phone. They never leave it, and Android removes them when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn reminders on, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone, using the phone's own alarm service. No server is involved. The app also keeps its reminder after the phone restarts (it asks Android to tell it when the phone has started). You can turn reminders off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

Permissions the app declares

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show the optional reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to set the reminder again after a restart.
  • WAKE_LOCK: to let the reminder appear on time while the phone is asleep.

Children

Because the app collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if online features are added), this page will be updated before the change ships, and the date above will change.

嘎嘎小岛：隐私政策

生效日期 2026-10-19 · 适用于安卓应用《嘎嘎小岛》（包名 com.shane.quackhaven）· English

简单说

《嘎嘎小岛》不收集、不发送、也不分享任何个人信息。游戏没有账号，没有广告，没有数据统计，没有内购；应用也不会申请联网权限。

存在你手机里的东西

  • 你的小岛：金币、建筑、居民、访客、装饰、每日目标和故事进度，保存在一个存档文件里。
  • 你的设置：音乐和音效音量、语言，以及提醒是否打开。

这两样都放在应用在手机上的私有存储里，不会离开你的手机。卸载应用或清除应用数据时，安卓会把它们一并删除。

提醒（可选）

如果你打开提醒，应用会向安卓申请显示通知的权限，并用手机自带的闹钟服务在手机本地安排提醒，不经过任何服务器。手机重启后，应用也会让安卓通知它，以便把提醒重新安排好。你随时可以在游戏设置或安卓的通知设置里关掉提醒。

应用声明的权限

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS：显示可选的提醒。
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED：手机重启后重新安排提醒。
  • WAKE_LOCK：手机休眠时也能按时显示提醒。

儿童

应用不向任何人收集任何信息，所以也不会向儿童收集。
